mpesa-match

Who paid, who didn't, in one minute

For chama treasurers, programme teams collecting fees and anyone selling tickets on a paybill. Paste the M-Pesa statement and the list of who should pay. It matches every payment by account number, phone or name, adds up part payments and shows who still owes.

Your statement stays on this device. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

8 members, 11 statement rows, one repeated, one failed, one stranger.
